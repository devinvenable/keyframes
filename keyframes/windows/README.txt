KEYFRAMES FOR WINDOWS
======================

1. Double-click Keyframes.exe to start.
2. Put your own images or videos in the images folder next to Keyframes.exe.
3. Restart Keyframes after changing the media folder.
4. Your computer keyboard works as a piano: Z-M is the lower octave and Q-P is
   the upper octave.  A connected MIDI controller also works automatically.
5. Press ESC to quit.

Supported images: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .bmp
Supported videos: .mp4, .avi, .mov, .mkv, .webm

Keep the images folder beside Keyframes.exe.  It is intentionally editable and
is not stored inside the program.
